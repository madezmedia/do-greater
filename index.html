Language guardrails

Say “I noticed a few things.” Not “audit,” not “retainer,” not “we found problems.”
Say “no ask attached.” Let the relationship reveal whether there is a formal lane later.
Say “what would help?” The useful product is listening well enough to leave with the real wishlist.
