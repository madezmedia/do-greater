The Four Quadrants at a Glance

MAD

Manageable Actions & Decisions
Break chaotic work into specific steps AI can help execute.

EASY

Exploratory AI for Simple Yield
Quick, low-stakes tasks with visible results in under 5 minutes.

ACMI

Persistent AI Memory & Context
Profiles, signals, and timelines that stop AI from starting from scratch.

AI Integration

Autonomous Workflows

Connected, tracked processes that operate continuously with minimal input.

Your 30-Day Roadmap

Week 1

Complete one EASY task daily. Build the muscle.

Week 2

Template your best EASY task so it's reusable.

Week 3

Seed your ACMI profile with at least 5 fields.

Week 4

Connect ACMI to one existing workflow.
Sample Prompt — Customer Reply "You are a customer success rep for a small bakery. A customer wrote: 'My birthday cake order is missing the extra sprinkles I paid for.' Write a warm, professional reply that apologizes, offers a fix, and includes a small goodwill gesture like a free cupcake next visit. Keep it under 100 words."
