# Do Greater — Brand Voice & Positioning Brief

## Mission
Help small business owners and operators turn fragmented systems into one repeatable growth engine by combining a simple MAD framework, EASY execution habits, ACMI accountability, and practical AI integration. Do Greater Charlotte runs these workshops at Sparkhouse with an ops-first, systems-first approach.

## Audience
Small business owners, ops leads, and founder-operators who:
- Wear multiple hats and have limited hours
- Already use some digital tools but feel like the stack is harder than the work
- Want momentum, not theoretical strategy
- Need implementation help they can use the same afternoon

## Unique Positioning
Most workshops sell either high-level strategy or narrow tool training. Do Greater sells sequenced systems: mindset/behavior first, tooling second. We connect the inner game — MAD + EASY + ACMI — with real AI integration that small business can actually adopt without a tech team.

## Brand Voice
- Direct, plain-spoken, no fluff
- Confident but not guru-adjacent; approachable, peer-like
- Action-oriented: steps, templates, and accountability by default
- Real-world examples over theory
- Light humor when it lands, never at the owner’s expense
- Respects their time: tight sections, takeaways, clear next actions

## On-Voice Examples
- "We don’t do motivation. We do systems."
- "Pick one thing, run it this week, then add AI."
- "Your best workflow is the one your team actually uses — not the demo."
- "If it’s not simple, it won’t stick. If it doesn’t stick, it didn’t happen."
