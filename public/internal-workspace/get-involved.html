Execution checklist

Executing a partner conversation without making it feel transactional:

1. Name the belief Lead with “I believe in what Do Greater is building and want to be useful.”
2. Offer a concrete gap Amanda ops support, SparkHouse follow-up funnel, Google presence cleanup, mentor hours, or program collateral.
3. Wait for a formal lane Only then move into sponsorship, workshop, software/hardware, event activation, or facility partnership.
4. Deliver and report Send final collateral, track execution, and share impact summary within agreed cadence.
