Founder Brief — Coach Mac

Coach Mac is time, not billable hours.

The founder relationship behind DO Greater: why Madez Media is in the room, what the formal workshop series is, and where the rest of the engagement actually lives — direct support for cohorts, the facility, and the work built for the kids.

Founder note

I love what Do Greater is doing, and I want to help — not as a vendor, just because I believe in it. The workshop series above is the formal, billed part of this relationship. This part isn’t that.

I know systems and AI well enough to be useful beyond a workshop room. I would rather put that time directly into the programs built for the kids — Start Upward, Designathon, wherever there’s an actual gap.

This isn’t a pitch. It is an open hand: if there’s a cohort that could use an extra mentor, or just time on the Sparkhouse floor, I’d rather be there than adjacent to it.
