Execution checklist

Executing a partner conversation:

1. Pick the lane Sponsorship, software/hardware donation, mentorship hours, event activation, or facility partnership.
2. Confirm deliverables Logo placement, mentor attendance, license count, coach hours, participant cap, and reporting terms.
3. Build the proposal Use the matching lane in Proposals. Import program facts so the offer is specific, not generic.
4. Deliver and report Send final collateral, track execution, and share impact summary within agreed cadence.
