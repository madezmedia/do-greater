Program/01 — Workshop Series

Connection workshop first. AI workshop when invited.

The adult workshop should make people feel the mission before they hear an offer: show them youth potential, ask what they would have built at 15, then let sponsorship become personal.

Work item: sparkhouse-do-greater-workshops-20260720. Next action is Module 1 curriculum draft plus SparkHouse venue confirmation.

Posture

Connection first

The workshop is available as a paid path, but the better first move is showing Coach Mac and Amanda that the mission is understood and the help is real.

Adult social story: sponsor the version of yourself who needed this.

Flip the social-story concept from learning to doing. DO Greater's story is youth turning ideas into impact. For adults, the workshop helps them see their own potential through the youth lens.

0:00-0:03

Start with a SparkHouse youth story

Use a short video or live share: a real young person, a real project, and a real outcome. The first move is emotional connection, not program description.

0:03-0:10

Reframe it

Ask: “What if you had this space, these tools, and this support when you were 15? What would you have built?”

0:10-0:25

Alternative-history exercise

Adults write the thing they would have created if SparkHouse existed for them, then pair-share. It is low-pressure, creative, and personal.

0:25-0:40

Flip it forward

“You cannot go back, but you can sponsor that version of yourself for someone else.” Introduce ambassador, mentor, volunteer, and sponsor tracks only after the room has arrived there.

0:40-0:55

Commitment circle

Close with: “Who here wants to be the person who gives a kid the space you wish you had?” Capture one concrete next step per person.

Why this works for adults

Nostalgic + aspirational. The exercise reaches backward into who they were and forward into who they can support.
Personal before transactional. Donate, volunteer, sponsor, and ambassador tracks feel like natural next steps, not pressure.
Mission memory. They leave with a story about themselves and a specific youth-support commitment tied to SparkHouse.

Inside an 80-minute session

0:00-0:10

Welcome + four quadrants

Business owners do not need platform theory first; we place the majority in MAD or EASY first and reframe ACMI and Integration as repeatable systems, not week-one goals.

0:10-0:30

EASY in action — first five wins

Customer reply draft, social captions batch, meeting-notes cleanup, FAQ, invoice follow-up — each under five minutes, done live before they leave the table.

0:30-0:50

ACMI — giving AI a memory

Owners draft the first persistent business profile: name, audience, voice, top services, “never say” rules, file examples, and customer context.

0:50-1:05

30-day roadmap + closing commitment

Personalized execution plan with weekly outcomes, owner accountability check-ins, and a curated Handoff pack for next steps.

1:05-1:15

Implementation pairing

Pair the roadmap with actual automation targets: follow-ups, content, review cycles, customer messaging, and measurement.

1:15-1:20

Q&A + specific asks

Owner-led question queue, with follow-up mapped to the next contact point.

What they leave with

Week 1

Five executable AI tasks completed live + a first-pass ACMI profile draft.

Immediate confidence

Week 2

Three-to-five recurring automations running with owner approval and guard rails.

Operational adoption

Week 3

Single source of truth for AI-facing inputs: voice file, brand file, customer file, offer file.

Repeatable brand memory

Week 4

Reviewed 30-day plan, updated campaign assets, next cohort or private follow-up scheduled.
