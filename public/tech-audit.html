Audit checklist

Entity + service clarity Organization, CSO, program names, and service definitions use consistent, indexable phrasing.
Page-level intent Every flagship page has one primary search intent, supporting subintents, and a linked conversion action.
Structured data present Organization, LocalBusiness, Event, EducationalProgram, and Article markup applied by content type.
Asset index Every downloadable one-pager, deck, and form has crawlable metadata, semantic link text, and revision date.
Quotable proof points Impact stats, cohort outcomes, sponsor testimonials, and partner logos available for citation.
Sentiment loop Survey, conversation feedback, and media mentions feed a monthly sentiment note for exec review.

Why it matters for DO Greater

Funders, sponsors, and city partners increasingly find programs through AI answers and trusted entity results. Without structured content and ongoing sentiment hygiene, the org risks being underrepresented in the decisions that shape its future.
