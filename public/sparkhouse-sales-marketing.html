Offer ladder

Start small. Move serious creators into deeper support.

Day Pass $20/day

Workspace, Wi-Fi, house drip, no commitment. Best for first visit, event follow-up, and low-friction trials.

Creator Club $129/mo

Coworking membership with all-day workspace, cafe access, studio hours, meeting-room access, and a weekly donut perk.

Founding 100 $99/mo

Limited founding Creator Club rate locked for one year. Use as urgency when spots are still available.

Dedicated Desk $299/mo

Permanent reserved desk, 24/7 access, house drip, espresso discount, studio access, and meeting room pricing.

Studio+ $199/mo

Creator Club plus production access, studio hours, meeting-room hours, and priority booking.

Private Office From $550

Solo offices from $550 and duo offices from $900. Best for operators who need a real address and consistent access.
