For Coach Mac · Do Not Duplicate

A few things worth a look before Friday.

Charlotte, NC · Sparkhouse Uptown · Fri, Jul 31

I'm coming through Friday to watch, not to sell anything. But I spend my days in exactly the stack Do Greater is running on, and a few things jumped out fast. Figured you'd rather hear it from someone in the room than find it in six months. No pitch below — just notes.

Sitting unclaimed, right now

$120,000/yr

Google's Ad Grant gives registered 501(c)(3)s $10k a month in free search ads. Do Greater qualifies and hasn't applied. That's a full year of paid reach, at zero media cost, left on the table.

Site

dogreater.org is working against you

WordPress on Divi, shared Bluehost hosting, no CDN or caching — grades out C- on load performance. Slower site, fewer people stick around to read it.

Search

Invisible to local search

Google Search Console was never set up, and the Google Business Profile is unclaimed. Anyone searching "youth entrepreneurship Charlotte" isn't finding you first.

Social

The audience is elsewhere

YouTube's sitting at ~4 subscribers. No TikTok, and that's exactly where the teens you serve already are. No LinkedIn, where the sponsor conversations happen.

Follow-up

No way to stay in touch

No lead capture, no volunteer signup funnel, no QR or text-to-join at events, no follow-up email after. People have a great time at Sparkhouse and then there's no thread to pull on Monday.

None of this needs a retainer to start moving — some of it's a form and an afternoon. I just didn't want you finding it on your own, later, the hard way. If you ever want to talk shop, founder to founder, the door's open.

— Mikey Madez Media
