← Do Greater A NOTE FOR COACH MAC
For Coach Mac

I love what you're building — and I think I can help.

Charlotte, NC · Sparkhouse Uptown · Fri, Jul 31

I run a small AI/systems shop here in Charlotte, and I didn't want to just show up Friday and introduce myself empty-handed. A few things below are just things I noticed and couldn't not mention — not a pitch, not an audit, just what I'd want someone to tell me if it were mine. No ask attached — just notes.

Designathon teaches kids to build with AI. Meanwhile —

$120,000/yr

Google's Ad Grant gives registered 501(c)(3)s $10k a month in free search ads. Do Greater qualifies and hasn't applied — the same tech instinct that runs Code the City hasn't been turned on the organization itself yet.

Already started for you

The Ad Grant, in three real steps

  1. Sign up for Google for Nonprofits, verify through TechSoup, then apply for the Ad Grant inside that portal.
  2. Wire up GA4 + Google Ads conversion tracking before the first campaign goes live — grant accounts get suspended for weak click-through (under 5%).
  3. Build campaigns around specific programs — Start Upward enrollment, Designathon registration, Sparkhouse Coffee — not a broad "donate" ad. Specific ad groups are what keep the account healthy.
Reach

The site works against the mission it's promoting

dogreater.org runs on WordPress + Divi, shared Bluehost hosting — C- on load performance. Every extra second is a parent or a kid bouncing before they see Start Upward or Designathon registration.

Discovery

Harder to find on Google than it should be

Search Console was never set up and the Google Business Profile is unclaimed. Someone searching "free entrepreneurship program for teens Charlotte" right now might not find Do Greater at all.

Audience

The 16-24 crowd isn't on the channels you're posting to

YouTube's sitting at ~4 subscribers. No TikTok — where that exact audience already lives. No LinkedIn, where the corporate-sponsor conversations happen.

Pipeline

Real foot traffic, no way to follow it

20,000 square feet, Sparkhouse Coffee, the showroom floor — people move through that building every day. No signup funnel, no QR, no post-visit email turns a coffee customer into a Start Upward applicant, or a grad into a returning mentor.

None of this needs a grant or a new hire — some of it's a form and an afternoon, some of it's a Tuesday with someone who's done it before. I just didn't want you finding these gaps the hard way. I love what you've built, and if there's ever a way I can actually help — not bill, just help — I'd genuinely like to.

— Mikey Madez Media
