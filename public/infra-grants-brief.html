Priority 01 · Free qualified search demand

Google Ad Grants is the easiest missed opportunity to operationalize.

Qualifying nonprofits can access up to $10,000 per month in in-kind Google Search ads. The grant does not guarantee spend; DO Greater has to make the website, tracking, landing pages, and campaigns good enough to use it.

Grant value model $10k/mo

Compliance gates 5%
  • Own and control the domain used in ads.
  • Keep HTTPS, working donation/contact flows, and clear mission content.
  • Avoid thin pages, broken links, generic keywords, and Quality Score 1-2 terms.
  • Maintain 5% monthly CTR and set meaningful conversion tracking.
  • Build at least 2 ad groups per campaign and at least 2 sitelink assets.
Verified source notes

Google lists Ad Grants as up to $10,000/month in Search ads; Google help documents require a high-quality website, mission-based keywords, 5% CTR compliance, and accurate conversion tracking where applicable.

Readiness matrix

The grant page should make funders feel the operation is already real.

The missing layer is not more adjectives. It is proof: governance, budget clarity, facility evidence, student outcomes, and a clean way for a reviewer to say yes.

Compliance drawer

On-ramp collateral

One clean packet should serve ads, sponsors, and grants.

01

Grant intake one-pager

Mission, population served, facility proof, funding need, budget menu, outcomes, contact, and EIN/admin details in one reviewer-ready PDF.

Use for foundation portals, city/county opportunities, corporate social responsibility teams, and warm sponsor intros.

02

Google Ads landing kit

Five landing pages: donate/sponsor, volunteer mentor, youth workshops, lab tour, and grant partner. Each has one conversion.

This prevents the ad grant from wasting traffic on pages that are too broad to convert.

03

Infrastructure budget menu

Line-item asks with "what it funds" and "what the sponsor gets": station, booth, cohort, workshop kit, event, software stack.

Turns abstract support into a concrete buying decision for local partners.

04

Impact evidence folder

Student work, photos, attendance sheets, quotes, program recaps, press, partner letters, and 90-second founder/lab videos.

Gives proposals a real proof layer and supports answer-engine visibility work.

30 / 60 / 90

Clean sweep execution plan

Days 1-30 · Eligibility + measurement

Make the floor usable.

  • Confirm Google for Nonprofits admin ownership and domain control.
  • Fix donation/contact/workshop conversion paths and GA4 events.
  • Create the grant intake one-pager and infrastructure budget menu.
  • Build five ad-specific landing page outlines before launching ads.
Days 31-60 · Campaigns + collateral

Launch the first demand loops.

  • Activate Google Ad Grants campaigns with two ad groups each.
  • Add sitelinks to Programs, Volunteer, Sponsor, Lab, and Contact.
  • Publish sponsor inventory and cohort scholarship packages.
  • Stand up the evidence folder for grant attachments and follow-ups.
Days 61-90 · Pipeline + reporting

Turn activity into funding proof.

  • Review CTR, conversion quality, search terms, and low-quality keywords weekly.
  • Route leads into proposal templates by donor, employer, funder, or city/county lane.
  • Create monthly sponsor reports from cohort, traffic, and conversion data.
  • Use the best-performing searches to guide new page copy and AEO/GEO content.
