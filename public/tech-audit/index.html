AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

Whether Google's AI Overviews, featured snippets, and voice assistants have anything to parse.

Zero structured data anywhere on the site

Confirmed

Checked the homepage and the About page directly for application/ld+json schema.org markup — none on either. No Organization schema, no Event schema for Designathon/camps, no FAQPage schema. Answer engines have nothing machine-readable to lift a fact from; they're working entirely off unstructured page text.

Gap
