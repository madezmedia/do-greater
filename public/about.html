A Note for Coach Mac ↗ Program Overview ↗

Mission

Do Greater Charlotte was founded to dismantle systemic barriers around creative technology, design, and entrepreneurship for underserved youth. The work is practical: access to equipment, funding for software, hands-on curriculum, and direct mentorship from Charlotte-area professionals.

It functions as a real workforce pipeline for Charlotte — kids who come through the CRTV Lab leave with skills, portfolios, and mentors, not just a certificate.

CRTV Lab facility

The space itself, briefly.

Location

2400 Beatties Ford Rd, Charlotte, NC 28216 — Sparkhouse Charlotte corridor.

Space

3,000+ sq ft hub with Apple Mac workstations, recording booths, collaborative studios, and 3D printers.

Curriculum

Music production, podcasting, digital design, 3D printing, entrepreneurship, and design thinking.

Delivery

Cohorts, workshops, and the annual Designathon — Code the City's sprint format.

Leadership

Coach Mac (William) leads Do Greater as Founder & Executive Director. Curriculum, community partnerships, and day-to-day CRTV Lab operations are run by a small Charlotte-based team.

Working with partners

What partners can offer

Sponsorship, mentorship, curriculum collaboration, facility access, and hands-on help. Typical shapes: a workshop series, cohort sponsorship, AI-integration support, or brand placement at public Designathon events.

What's genuinely useful: funding, hardware, software licenses, mentors, venue partnerships, and people willing to show up for the relationship, not just write a check.
