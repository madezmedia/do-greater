Mission

Do Greater Charlotte was founded to dismantle systemic barriers around creative technology, design, and entrepreneurship for underserved youth. The work is practical: access to equipment, funding for software, hands-on curriculum, and direct mentorship from Charlotte-area professionals.

Sales angle: framing DO Greater as a workforce pipeline and community-resource multiplier. Partners aren't donors only—they get access to a curated coalition, youth creativity, and measurable impact reporting.

Partner positioning

What we offer partners

Sponsorship, mentorship slots, NEAR Protocol / web3 exposure, curriculum collaboration, facility access, and zero-overhead done-for-you activation. Typical tracks: workshop series, cohort sponsorship, AI-integration support, and brand placement at public Designathon events.

What we need: funding, hardware, software licenses, mentors, venue partnerships, and operational exposure from founders who want relationship over transaction.
