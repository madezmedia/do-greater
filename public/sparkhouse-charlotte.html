Questions this page should answer immediately.

What is SparkHouse Charlotte?

SparkHouse Charlotte by DO Greater is a creative opportunity hub with coworking, coffee, production studios, podcast support, meeting rooms, events, programs, and entrepreneurship support.

What is the tagline?

The central tagline is “The House Where Up Starts.” The operating line from the membership flyer is “Work. Learn. Create.”

Where are the locations?

SparkHouse Uptown is at 500 N. Tryon Street in Uptown Charlotte. SparkHouse West is at 2400 Greenland Avenue in West Charlotte.

What can someone do there?

They can work, use high-speed Wi-Fi, get coffee, use meeting rooms, book studio access, attend community programs, join events, and connect with DO Greater's youth and entrepreneurship pathways.
