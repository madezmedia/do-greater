A few notes from Mikey

Where Do Greater shows up online, and where it doesn't yet.

A few honest things I noticed poking around — not a formal audit, just what stood out.

A Note for Coach Mac ↗ Talk It Through ↗

The clearest one

I searched "best youth entrepreneurship programs Charlotte NC teens" — the kind of question a parent, or an AI assistant answering on a parent's behalf, would actually ask. Do Greater doesn't show up at all right now, even with a $4.6M building and real backing from Ally Charitable Foundation and Novant Health. Six smaller programs do show up.

That's fixable — it mostly comes down to a few structural things below, not anything about the mission itself.

A few things underneath it

Google Business Profile

Currently unclaimed — an easy, free fix that helps with local search right away.

Search Console

Never set up, so there's no visibility yet into how people are actually finding (or not finding) the site.

Site speed

Shared hosting plus the current site builder makes for a slower load than the mission deserves — every extra second is someone bouncing before they see Start Upward or Designathon.

Where the kids actually are

YouTube's at a handful of subscribers. No TikTok yet, where the 16–24 crowd already spends time; no LinkedIn, where sponsor conversations tend to happen.

None of this is urgent or expensive to start on — happy to walk through any of it whenever's useful.
