Getting Started with AI for Small Business

An 80-minute, hands-on session that gets Charlotte small-business owners from "I've heard of ChatGPT" to a dated 30-day AI plan for their own business — no developer, no budget, no prior AI experience required.

Venue Sparkhouse Charlotte Ops contact Amanda Format 4 × 20-min blocks + buffer Cohort 12 min · 18 optimal · 25 cap

The framework: four quadrants, in order

Every session, every task, every roadmap item gets sorted into one of these — deliberately in this sequence, since that's the order participants actually move through it.

MAD

Manageable Actions & Decisions. Break chaotic work into specific steps AI can help execute.

EASY

Exploratory AI for Simple Yield. Quick, low-stakes wins with visible results in under 5 minutes.

ACMI

Persistent AI Memory. Profiles, signals, and timelines so AI stops starting from scratch.

AI Integration

Autonomous workflows. Connected, tracked processes that run with minimal input.

Inside an 80-minute session

Workshop 01 — the entry point every new cohort starts with.

0:00–0:20

Welcome & the four quadrants

Orient the room to MAD / EASY / ACMI / AI Integration. Each owner writes one real, repetitive task from their own business on a sticky note and places it on the board.

"Notice how most sticky notes land in MAD or EASY first. That's normal — ACMI and AI Integration are where we move things over time, not week one."
0:20–0:40

EASY in action — your first five wins

Live, on their own laptop: a customer reply draft, a batch of social captions, meeting-notes cleanup, an FAQ, or an invoice follow-up — each under 5 minutes, done for real before they leave the room.

0:40–1:00

ACMI — giving AI a memory

Most AI interactions are amnesic — brand voice, customer list, pricing, all forgotten every session. Owners draft the first section of their own persistent business profile: name, audience, voice, top services, "never say" rules.

1:00–1:20

30-day roadmap & closing commitment

Every owner leaves with a dated, four-week plan and one accountability partner picked from the room — not a stack of theory, a specific Monday-morning task.

What they leave with

The roadmap structure every participant fills in before they walk out.

Week 1

One EASY task daily — build the muscle.

Week 2

Template the best EASY task so it's reusable.

Week 3

Seed an ACMI profile — at least 5 fields.

Week 4

Connect ACMI to one existing workflow.
