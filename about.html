Our Mission & History

Building Creative Confidence in Charlotte's Youth

Do Greater Charlotte was founded with a singular vision: to dismantle systemic barriers to creative technology and entrepreneurship for underserved youth.

The CRTV Lab Vision

Located in the heart of Charlotte, NC, the CRTV Lab (Creative Technology Lab) is a 3,000+ sq ft space equipped with state-of-the-art Apple workstations, music recording equipment, 3D printers, and collaborative design studios.

Here, middle and high school students transition from technology consumers to digital creators and tech leaders.
