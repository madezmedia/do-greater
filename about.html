Our History & Mission

Unlocking Creative Confidence in Charlotte's Youth

Do Greater Charlotte was founded to dismantle systemic barriers to creative technology, design, and entrepreneurship for underserved youth.

The CRTV Lab Facility

Located in Charlotte, NC, the CRTV Lab (Creative Technology Lab) is a 3,000+ sq ft hub equipped with Apple Mac workstations, digital audio recording booths, 3D printers, and collaborative studios.

Students transition from technology consumers into digital creators, brand builders, and future tech entrepreneurs.

Led From Charlotte, For Charlotte

Do Greater is run by a Charlotte-based team of educators, mentors, and community partners — backed by Ally Charitable Foundation and Novant Health, and built alongside the families and schools it serves.
