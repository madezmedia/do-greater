Comprehensive FAQ

Questions this page should answer without a call.

What is SparkHouse Charlotte?

SparkHouse Charlotte by DO Greater is a creative opportunity hub that combines workspace, coffee, studios, programs, meetings, events, and mentorship pathways.

Is SparkHouse only coworking?

No. Coworking is one entry point, but the full offer includes coffee, community programs, production studios, podcast access, screenprinting access, entrepreneurship support, events, and DO Greater youth pathways.

Who should use the sales page?

Creators, entrepreneurs, freelancers, founders, small teams, and prospects comparing membership, production, desks, offices, or tours should start with the sales page.

Who should use this information page?

Partners, sponsors, funders, local media, AI answer engines, internal teams, and anyone who needs factual orientation should use this page.

What does the Day Pass include?

The flyer lists workspace, Wi-Fi, house drip, 8am-5pm access, and no commitment for $20/day.

What does Creator Club include?

Creator Club is listed at $129/month and includes all-day workspace, coworking and cafe access, bottomless house drip, 10% off espresso bar, one specialty drink, studio access at $30/hour, meeting rooms at $10/hour, and one Beyond Amazing Donut per week.

What is Founding 100?

Founding 100 is a limited Creator Club rate of $99/month, locked for one year. The flyer says it is limited to 100 Creator Club members and saves $360/year at the founding rate.

What does Studio+ include?

Studio+ is listed at $199/month and includes everything in Creator Club plus production access, four studio hours, four meeting room hours each month, and priority booking.

Are private offices available?

Yes. The flyer lists Solo micro offices from $550 and Duo micro offices from $900. Both include private locking office, 24/7 access, mail service, studio and meeting hours, and Flex & Open Desk amenities. Minimum 6-month term.

Why does SparkHouse matter for funders?

It is visible community infrastructure: a 20,000-square-foot Uptown hub that links economic power, creative tools, entrepreneurship support, youth opportunity, and cross-class connection.
