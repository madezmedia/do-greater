What the grader found

The site is accessible, but not legible to AI.

Crawler access is not the blocker. The blocker is missing entity structure, fragmented naming, thin rendered content, and no authoritative answer package for search or AI systems to quote.

Evidence map

Where the current web presence breaks down.

01 · High

AI cold knowledge points to the wrong organization.

When queried cold, the model described a regional data-collaboration nonprofit tied to Quality of Life Explorer work, not DO Greater's youth creativity, design, entrepreneurship, and technology mission.

02 · High

Structured data is effectively zero.

Homepage, About, Programs, News, Summer Camps, and Designathon pages showed no JSON-LD for Organization, Nonprofit, Event, Course, FAQ, or LocalBusiness context.

03 · High

Some pages are invisible to plain crawlers.

Reported examples include near-zero visible text on pages such as /fall-programs/ and /give/, which makes the content fragile for search snippets and AI retrieval.

04 · Medium

The flagship space has naming drift.

Creative Lab Uptown, Sparkhouse Charlotte Uptown, Sparkhouse Charlotte by DO Greater, and Sparkhouse Coffee need one entity hierarchy and one canonical description.

Priority findings

Ranked by what blocks funders, sponsors, and AI answers.

High

Publish an authoritative entity page.

Create one crawlable page that defines DO Greater Charlotte, Sparkhouse Charlotte, Coach Mac, Creative Lab Uptown, mission, location, programs, and partner pathways in consistent terms.

High

Add schema across the core site.

Ship Organization + Nonprofit schema on the homepage, EducationalProgram / Event schema on program pages, Article schema for briefs, and FAQ schema for partner-facing answers.

High

Replace thin rendered pages with server-visible content.

Each fundraising, program, and facility page needs indexable HTML copy, one H1, descriptive H2s, and meaningful link text before design enhancements load.

Medium

Standardize the Sparkhouse architecture.

Lock naming as DO Greater as parent, Sparkhouse Charlotte as platform, Creative Lab Uptown as facility, and Sparkhouse Coffee / studios as programs or spaces.

Medium

Create /llms.txt and a crawler source pack.

Point AI systems to About, Programs, Spring 2026, New Creative Lab, Founder Brief, Infra + Grants, and Contact with one-sentence descriptions for each.

Clean sweep plan

30 / 60 / 90 days to become quotable.

30 days

Make the truth crawlable.

  • One canonical DO Greater entity page
  • One Sparkhouse / Creative Lab naming map
  • Homepage, About, Programs schema launch
  • Remove Hello World and empty sitemap clutter
60 days

Build answer assets.

  • /llms.txt with priority pages and summaries
  • FAQ blocks for donors, partners, families, and students
  • Program pages with dates, eligibility, outcomes, and forms
  • Founder and facility briefs rewritten as indexable articles
90 days

Prove and monitor.

  • Monthly AI answer checks in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity
  • Partner quote and press backlink campaign
  • Sentiment notes for sponsors and grant reviewers
  • Dashboard for indexed pages, schema coverage, and AI citations
