A little free money worth knowing about.

Nothing here needs a grant writer or a new hire — just worth flagging.

A Note for Coach Mac ↗ Talk It Through ↗

Google Ad Grants

Google gives registered 501(c)(3)s up to $10,000/month in free Search ads. Do Greater already qualifies, and it doesn't look like it's been claimed yet — that's real, recurring money for something like Start Upward enrollment or Designathon registration.

It's not fully automatic: the account needs Google for Nonprofits + TechSoup verification, some basic conversion tracking so the campaigns stay compliant, and ad groups built around specific programs rather than a generic "donate" ad. All doable in an afternoon or two, not a project.

A few others worth a look

Google Workspace for Nonprofits

Free or deeply discounted Docs, Drive, and admin email for the team.

Microsoft & TechSoup

Discounted Microsoft 365 and a wide catalog of discounted software/hardware once nonprofit status is verified.

Cloud & hosting credits

AWS, Google Cloud, GitHub, and Cloudflare all offer nonprofit credit programs — useful if the site or student projects ever need more infrastructure.

YouTube Nonprofit Program

Extra tools for nonprofit channels — worth pairing with the student-story and lab-tour content Do Greater already has.

Happy to help with any of this directly, or just answer questions — no pressure either way.
