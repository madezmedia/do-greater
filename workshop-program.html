Inside an 80-minute session

0:00–0:20

Welcome + four quadrants

Business owners do not need platform theory first; we place the majority in MAD or EASY first and reframe ACMI and Integration as repeatable systems, not week-one goals.

0:20–0:40

EASY in action — first five wins

Customer reply draft, social captions batch, meeting-notes cleanup, FAQ, invoice follow-up — each under five minutes, done live before they leave the table.

0:40–1:00

ACMI — giving AI a memory

Owners draft the first persistent business profile: name, audience, voice, top services, “never say” rules, file examples, and customer context.

1:00–1:20

30-day roadmap + closing commitment

Personalized execution plan with weekly outcomes, owner accountability check-ins, and a curated Handoff pack for next steps.

1:20–1:40

Implementation pairing

Pair the roadmap with actual automation targets: follow-ups, content, review cycles, customer messaging, and measurement.

1:40–1:50

Q&A + specific asks

Owner-led question queue, with follow-up mapped to the next contact point.

1:50–2:00

Closing action cards

Each attendee leaves with a dated 30-day roadmap and a Handoff card for the next conversation.
